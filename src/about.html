04 / BEFORE WE BEGIN

A little more clarity.

Do I need a complete specification?

A clear problem and a rough goal are enough to start a conversation. Discovery helps turn what you know about your business into a practical scope and a set of priorities.

Can we start with a small first version?

Yes. A prototype, proof of concept, or focused minimum viable product can help test an idea before committing to a larger build. The right first step depends on what you need to learn.

Can you work with our existing software?

Our services include modernization, API integration, and workflow automation. We can review the current system, its available interfaces, and its constraints to identify a suitable approach.

How are cost and timing determined?

Scope, integrations, design needs, and technical complexity all affect a project. We start by understanding those details and agreeing on priorities before proposing an estimate and delivery plan.

What happens after launch?

Ongoing work can include maintenance, performance improvements, and new features. Support and the next development steps should be agreed as part of the project plan.
